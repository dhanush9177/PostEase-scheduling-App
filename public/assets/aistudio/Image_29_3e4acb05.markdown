## Brand & Style

This design system is built for fast-moving digital creators, viral editors, and social media managers who operate at the bleeding edge of the creator economy. The visual voice is electric, focused, and hyper-modern: it combines the immersion of high-end video production suites with the kinetic energy of short-form algorithmic feeds.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses deep, dark-mode minimalism with refined glassmorphism and electric chromatic accents. Interfaces should feel like a precision studio instrument—uncluttered canvas areas framed by luminous neon accents that signal AI-driven generation, live multi-platform publishing states, and engagement spikes. The experience prioritizes low cognitive friction during high-velocity asset management, while celebrating viral milestones with vibrant bursts of violet and neon magenta.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast against an ultra-dark canvas to maximize focus on media assets and status metrics:

- **Canvas & Obsidian Backgrounds:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#0A0D14` (Void Slate)
  - Surface Raised / Card Fill: `#121624` (Deep Obsidian)
  - Glass Border & Divider: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Elevated Container / Hover: `#1A2035`

- **Kinetic Accents:**
  - Primary Accent: `#8B5CF6` (Electric Violet) for core platform actions, active states, and AI engine status.
  - Secondary Accent: `#EC4899` (Neon Magenta) for video generation cues, high-engagement trends, and primary triggers.
  - Tertiary Accent: `#06B6D4` (Cyber Cyan) for performance metrics, analytics, and temporal markers.
  - Linear Brand Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #EC4899 50%, #06B6D4 100%)` for hero AI triggers, primary FABs, and viral metric highlights.

- **Platform Network Signatures (Strict Utility):**
  - WhatsApp: `#25D366`
  - Instagram: `#E1306C`
  - YouTube: `#FF0000`
  - Facebook: `#1877F2`

- **Text & Foreground Hierarchy:**
  - Foreground Primary: `#FFFFFF` (100% opacity)
  - Foreground Secondary: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
  - Foreground Dimmed: `#64748B` (Slate 500)

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative editorial tone balanced with crisp functional clarity:

- **Display & Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural confidence with its wide geometric stances, open counters, and contemporary cut. It is reserved for top-tier metrics, screen titles, reel draft names, and AI prompt headings.
- **Body & Functional Labels:** Inter ensures legibility across dense timeline tracks, multi-account handles, caption previews, and schedule logs. High-density information relies on tracking adjustments for immediate scanning.
- **Metric Displays:** Numbers on dashboard analytics, countdown timers, and reel performance indicators enforce tabular figure alignments (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter during real-time tracking updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is tailored primarily for handheld mobile viewports (390px to 428px base widths) while remaining fluid across folding displays and tablets:

- **Mobile Viewport Structure:** Standard 4-column fluid layout with a base margin of `1rem` (16px) and gutters of `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Expanded Devices / Tablets:** Transitions to an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters, allowing side-by-side video timeline editing and analytics feeds.
- **Vertical Metric Rhythm:** Built upon an intentional 4px base coordinate matrix (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Vertical rhythm dictates `space-md` between adjacent card containers and `space-xl` between logical workflow tiers (e.g., Timeline Scheduler vs. Platform Selector).
- **Thumb Reach Zone:** High-frequency interactions (AI Magic Cut triggers, scheduling FABs, platform selector chips) anchor strictly to the bottom 40% of the mobile viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism anchored on dark obsidian strata:

- **Base Layer (Ground Zero):** `#0A0D14` serves as the infinite canvas. No shadows or borders.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Containers):** Translucent backdrop fills using `#121624` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a crisp stroke of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Pickers, Sheets & Modals):** Background fill `#1A2035` at 92% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, bounded by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Supported by a diffused directional cast: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1)`.
- **Luminescent Accent Glows:** Active elements (such as executing AI generation nodes or selected social targets) emit a low-opacity ambient halo: `0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)` or `0 0 20px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.35)`. Harsh, opaque drop shadows are prohibited.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances soft ergonomic interaction points with technical digital frames:

- Standard structural cards, media clip containers, and bottom-sheet panels use a consistent base radius of `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Interactive controls, status badges, metric chips, and form elements leverage full pill configurations (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to invite thumb engagement and mimic mobile video scrubber tags.
- Nested timeline items and micro media asset thumbnails drop to `0.5rem` (`rounded`) to preserve layout efficiency and avoid visual clash inside parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary AI Action:** Full pill button styled with the kinetic gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6, #EC4899)`), white text (`label-lg`), and subtle perimeter glow. Pressed states scale down slightly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Utility Action:** Obsidian fill (`#121624`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, foreground white.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#94A3B8` icon/text, switching to `#FFFFFF` on active press.

### Platform Micro-Badges & Indicators
- Compact pills (20px to 24px height) pairing high-contrast platform brand tones with solid white iconography.
- Active synchronization states feature an animated outer breathing ring matching the specific platform color (e.g., `#25D366` pulse for WhatsApp queueing).

### Glass Cards & Timeline Items
- Scheduled queue items use the standard glass container (`#121624` with 16px backdrop blur).
- The left edge incorporates an absolute vertical indicator bar (3px width) colored according to scheduling status (Cyan = Queued, Violet = Processing AI, Green = Published, Pink = Action Needed).

### Pill Toggles & Platform Selectors
- Multi-target selector row composed of horizontal auto-scrolling pills. 
- Inactive: dark surface with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` border and muted monochromatic logo.
- Selected: full-saturation platform branding with subtle neon underglow and a trailing white micro-checkmark.

### Creator Metric Tiles
- Compact two-column glass grid displaying real-time metrics (Views, Retention, Viral Velocity).
- Top: uppercase tracking label (`label-sm`, `#94A3B8`).
- Middle: large numeric output (`code-metric`, `#FFFFFF`).
- Bottom: trend delta indicator pill (`+18.4%` in Emerald or Cyber Cyan).

### Input Fields & Prompt Consoles
- ReelCraft AI Prompt Bar: Docked to bottom safe-area, elevated with frosted glass (`#1A2035`), pill contour, featuring dynamic inline tokens for styling (e.g., `#Cinematic`, `#AutoSubtitles`, `#HookFormula`).
- Inputs use `#0A0D14` backgrounds when embedded inside modal cards, maintaining explicit `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` boundaries.
